One day of world news on one page: 38 stories from Tuesday 24 November 2026, led by the Houthi drone hit on Riyadh's PIF tower, the probe of Shanghai party chief Chen Jining, and Huawei's guilty verdict on 10 of 12 counts. Built with the winning front-page template — 3 hero scenes, 10 numbered cards, an 8-number strip, a real lat/lon world map with 13 pins, and a DATE CHECK that keeps every story on the right day.

Render: `python3 main.py` (Python + PIL only) writes `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, deterministic at 1024×1024.
